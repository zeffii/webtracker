Sequencer

Press Start to create the audio engine.


  

  
Click the grid, then: arrows move, Tab next lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 rows, Space play/stop,
    Enter inserts a pattern, Z–M and Q–P play notes, [ ] octave, 1 note off, ` note cut,
    0–9 A–F overwrite the character under the cursor (an empty cell fills with zeros), Delete
    clears the cell. Left and Right move one character. Edits are heard on the next tick.


  
Live sliders (lead lane). Where the pattern writes a value, it wins.
