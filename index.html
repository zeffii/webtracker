Sequencer

Press Start to create the audio engine.

Samples and instruments

Audio files are mixed down to mono. A loop start and end of 0 means no loop.

The waveform shows the sample chosen with its Waveform button, with the loop between the two markers. Drag a marker to move it, drag across the waveform to make a loop, double-click to clear it.

Patcher

Drag a node to move it. Drag from an output dot (right side) to an input dot (left side) to wire them; a new wire replaces the one already feeding that input. Click a wire or a node and press Delete to remove it. In the panel, sliders change a parameter and "expose" adds it to the instrument's pattern columns. Changing the wiring or the exposed list swaps the instrument in live and clears undo.

Arrangement


  

  
Up/Down move in time, Left/Right change lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 ticks. Enter opens the pattern under
    the cursor in the grid, or inserts a new one; I inserts a new pattern on top. Two hex digits place that pattern
    (see the list) and jump to its end. N clones, S slices at the cursor, + and - change length by 16,
    , and . move it up or down a tick (< and > sixteen), Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes,
    W sets a waypoint and Shift+W restores it. Space plays.


  
Pattern grid

  

  

  
Click the grid, then (Esc returns to the arrangement): arrows move, Tab next lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 rows, Space play/stop,
    Enter inserts a pattern, Z–M and Q–P play notes, [ ] octave, 1 note off, ` note cut,
    0–9 A–F overwrite the character under the cursor (an empty cell fills with zeros), Delete
    clears the cell. Left and Right move one character. Edits are heard on the next tick.


  
Live sliders (lead lane). Where the pattern writes a value, it wins.
