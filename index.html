Sequencer

Press Start to create the audio engine.

Arrangement


  

  
Left/Right move in time, Up/Down change lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 ticks. Enter opens the pattern under
    the cursor in the grid, or inserts a new one; I inserts a new pattern on top. Two hex digits place that pattern
    (see the list) and jump to its end. N clones, S slices at the cursor, + and - change length by 16,
    , and . move it a tick (< and > sixteen), Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes,
    W sets a waypoint and Shift+W restores it. Space plays.


  
Pattern grid

  

  

  
Click the grid, then (Esc returns to the arrangement): arrows move, Tab next lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 rows, Space play/stop,
    Enter inserts a pattern, Z–M and Q–P play notes, [ ] octave, 1 note off, ` note cut,
    0–9 A–F overwrite the character under the cursor (an empty cell fills with zeros), Delete
    clears the cell. Left and Right move one character. Edits are heard on the next tick.


  
Live sliders (lead lane). Where the pattern writes a value, it wins.
