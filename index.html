In the arrangement, M on a row mutes that lane from there until the next M further down (shown dimmed, with m and u marking the changes). K steps the colour of the pattern under the cursor through 16 pastel presets and back to the automatic colour (Shift+K goes backwards). Shift+M silences the whole lane, whatever its marks say, and darkens it further; press it again to bring the lane back.

      0–9 A–F overwrite the character under the cursor (an empty cell fills with zeros), Delete
      clears the cell. Left and Right move one character. Edits are heard on the next tick.
      Dimmed rows are past the end of the pattern.

Drag a node to move it. Drag from an output dot (right side) to an input dot (left side) to
          wire them. An input can take several wires, which are added together; use a mix node when you want a ratio. Blue wires carry audio, amber wires carry control signals (envelopes, lfos, triggers), and heavier purple wires carry a stereo pair (a mono wire into a stereo input feeds both channels; stereo into a mono input is averaged). Right-click the graph, or double-click empty space, to insert a node. Click a wire or a node and press Delete
          to remove it. In the panel, sliders change a parameter and "expose" adds it to the instrument's pattern
          columns. Changing the wiring or the exposed list swaps the instrument in live and clears undo.

      

    

  


  

    

    

    
Up/Down move in time, Left/Right change lane, PgUp/PgDn 16 ticks. Enter opens the pattern under
      the cursor in the grid, or inserts a new one; I inserts a new pattern on top. Two hex digits place that pattern
      (see the list) and jump to its end. N clones, S slices at the cursor, + and - change length by 16,
      , and . move it up or down a tick (< and > sixteen), Delete removes it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes,
      W sets a waypoint and Shift+W restores it. Space plays.
